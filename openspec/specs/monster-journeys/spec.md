# monster-journeys Specification

## Purpose
Own real monster travel passages, their persistent identity and exact elapsed settlement without endpoint presence or autonomous clock advancement.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Mapped individual escape commits a real adjacent departure
Mapped individual flee SHALL require an affordable legal adjacent route at its action occurrence and atomically commit fled identity, detachment and durable departure without moving its remaining opponents.

#### Scenario: Real resolver departure
- **WHEN** one of A+A+B wins its existing agility contest with a lawful affordable neighbor
- **THEN** only that ID leaves source presence and its movement group, retains home/site/quest ownership and begins one journey at the actual round offset; remaining enemies continue

#### Scenario: Route or reserve failure
- **WHEN** no legal affordable neighbor exists, including when there is no eligible pursuer
- **THEN** the attempt fails without departure/detachment/fled changes, apart from ordinary action costs and time; corrupt state refuses by name

#### Scenario: Outside normal range
- **WHEN** the only lawful escape edge crosses the normal activity boundary
- **THEN** escape can use it without a new immunity flag or changing home ownership

#### Scenario: Explicit abstract calculation
- **WHEN** a standalone non-world battlefield resolves innate flee
- **THEN** its explicit abstract context changes battlefield eligibility without claiming world relocation; mapped callers cannot silently fall back

### Requirement: Journey passage preserves elapsed resources and actual presence
A durable monster journey SHALL settle committed travel physiology once through player-driven time, remain absent from both endpoints until lawful arrival and retain identity while an arrival is temporarily blocked.

#### Scenario: Unobserved partition and restart
- **WHEN** a journey advances in one interval or equivalent partitions with reload midway
- **THEN** same IDs/resources/progress result, no inactive room is created and neither endpoint lists it before arrival

#### Scenario: Protected arrival
- **WHEN** a destination becomes an active or historically protected battle cell before arrival
- **THEN** the same living home-owned journey waits and retries positively without reinforcement or a second traversal charge; waiting follows ordinary applicable posture

#### Scenario: No recursive time
- **WHEN** an enemy flees during one action and other enemies continue
- **THEN** the journey starts at that action round offset and progresses only through later player-driven clock settlement, without an added global round per fleeing actor

#### Scenario: Deferred combat physiology transfers once
- **WHEN** an actual monster flees after two unpaid six-second rounds, travels and later remains idle across partitioned catch-up and reload
- **THEN** its committed reserve pays 12 seconds of actual combat before passage, only newly elapsed journey/idle intervals afterwards, and no old terminal or generic settlement repeats those intervals

### Requirement: Journey operations restore complete state on failure and replay
Journey operations SHALL commit receipts and every touched location, ownership, resource and encounter surface atomically, restoring durable state and live caches on failure and applying committed replay at most once.

#### Scenario: Late failure
- **WHEN** a fault occurs after actual detachment, registration, physiology or session writes
- **THEN** reload and live room/Attribute/coordinate caches match source state; retry repeats deterministic selection and emits no rolled-back success

#### Scenario: Reengagement
- **WHEN** the player follows an escaped monster after its real arrival
- **THEN** the same identity and remaining resources can be engaged immediately with no cooldown or opponent blacklist

### Requirement: Activity ordering protects encounters across deferred time
Activity settlement SHALL process all due facts chronologically in stable identity order, apply lawful arrivals and births before contacts and idle departures, and honor encounter protection at actual occurrence times.

#### Scenario: Same tick contact precedence
- **WHEN** lawful arrivals, births and an idle departure become due together
- **THEN** arrival and existing site-before-regional births complete before contact evaluation, which precedes idle departures

#### Scenario: Historical protection
- **WHEN** terminal clock catch-up passes an earlier active battle after the session closes
- **THEN** participants never wander and outside groups neither depart into nor arrive inside the protected cell; a lawful escaped member can leave from its real departure tick

#### Scenario: Newly due facts
- **WHEN** an earlier arrival schedules a decision within the same long advance
- **THEN** that decision is processed at its own tick and equal elapsed partitions preserve positions, sequence, counts and physiology
