# hostile-field-contact Specification

## Purpose
Admit authored aggressive field contacts under deterministic authority and expose actual actionable or terminal combat state without arrival grace.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Authored hostile contact admits immediate ordinary combat
An eligible authored aggression contact SHALL immediately admit one normal whole-cell encounter at actual arrival, birth, condition boundary or pre-action contact, without grace, free damage or an extra opening turn.

#### Scenario: Either arrival and birth
- **WHEN** a player or aggressive monster arrives, or a hostile member is born, at an idle co-located party
- **THEN** one session contains every living local monster in stable individual order, including passive/site/quest members; awake actors retain normal draft choices

#### Scenario: Passive and defended conditions
- **WHEN** only passive members are present or a defended/time condition is false
- **THEN** no hostile session starts; when an explicit condition becomes true its lawful contact is admitted immediately

#### Scenario: Arrival and read authority
- **WHEN** a physical traversal is still in progress or a view/preview/reconnect read runs
- **THEN** no endpoint contact or read-triggered admission occurs; committed arrival evaluates contact before another exploration action or route step

#### Scenario: No reengagement immunity
- **WHEN** either side lawfully returns to co-location after an escape
- **THEN** current applicable contact can immediately create a fresh encounter, including previous opponents, using actual occupants and remaining resources

#### Scenario: Explicit staged encounter
- **WHEN** a guild examination or staged non-field encounter admits its designated opponents
- **THEN** ordinary field aggression does not widen that roster or replace its existing outcome policy

### Requirement: Hostile sleep interruption preserves accepted body state
Hostile admission SHALL interrupt voluntary sleep through its owner without refilling reserve, preserve forced-sleep receipt and threshold, and keep voluntary initiation and movement sleep gates intact.

#### Scenario: Voluntary receipt
- **WHEN** contact interrupts voluntary sleep with partial actual recovery
- **THEN** the receipt becomes interrupted at the accepted tick, posture wakes without reserve refill and old continuation cannot resume

#### Scenario: Forced receipt
- **WHEN** hostile contact reaches a forced sleeper below its authored wake threshold
- **THEN** its posture and receipt remain forced; awake companions can act under existing control rules and no voluntary sleep gate is weakened
