# human-combat-calibration Specification

## Purpose
Define record conditional human calibration and verify real gear/restrictions in bounded resolver probes as observable deterministic behavior with explicit rejection and acceptance boundaries.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Human calibration uses real resolver gear restrictions and bounded outcome evidence
Human-only calibration SHALL keep approved section 8 tables/conditions as conditional historical projected evidence and record real gear/restriction/persistent-host smoke separately. Calibration SHALL exercise real Evennia objects, usable lineage/proficiency, registered items/accessories, ActionResolver, initiative, costs/upkeep and run_round at full pools with simulation markers under a 200-round bound.

#### Scenario: Real restrictions
- **WHEN** unchanged persistent host wears actual exam kit
- **THEN** all restriction consumers match and immutable normal records survive terminal restoration

#### Scenario: Marker discipline
- **WHEN** probe resolves skills/rounds
- **THEN** no growth/unlock drift or ordinary kill reward occurs

#### Scenario: No tuning or new species
- **WHEN** calibration probes are built
- **THEN** no combat math duplication, elf/beastfolk target or new species is introduced; the approved ratio/weapon/tactical calibration records initial and selected values, and permits only observed-outcome-supported authored tuning through its owning balance slice

### Requirement: Evidence separates defeat retreat support safety and unverified projections
Evidence SHALL record seeds/build/item/profile/backend/engine revision and monster defeats, retreats, human losses, all-standing, unfinished, rejected actions and round medians. Seeds 0-3 SHALL bound focused probes; original 0-15/0-7 tables SHALL keep their qualifications. A retreat SHALL NOT establish kill/loot/hunt credit; high/calamity probes SHALL remain unshipped and non-universal. A real-kit mismatch SHALL block acceptance until the owning change diagnoses it.

#### Scenario: Retreat
- **WHEN** monster behavior policy flees
- **THEN** encounter escape is reported separately from zero-HP defeat with no inferred kill credit

#### Scenario: Upper boundary
- **WHEN** S succeeds in a sampled high-upper matchup
- **THEN** report does not promise universal S victory or all-standing A-party safety

#### Scenario: Diagnosis preserves approved values
- **WHEN** a real-kit mismatch is diagnosed in the owning change
- **THEN** the diagnosis proceeds without silently altering approved values

### Requirement: Equipment growth evidence uses real construction and committed reload
Equipment-growth smoke SHALL use real entity construction, registered gear, ordinary grant/equip rules and committed reload. Evidence SHALL record item keys, initial/selected multipliers and budgets, true stats, defenses, fixed rolls, backend and engine revision. Independent fixed arithmetic controls SHALL establish placement separately from registry values.

#### Scenario: Required independent controls
- **WHEN** K=40 and defense=20 are evaluated with premitigation attack60, Defend0.35 and physical weapon1.5
- **THEN** evidence records ordinary40, defended26 and weapon60, with matching magic unaffected by weapon factor

#### Scenario: Durable provenance
- **WHEN** a factor is observed after equipping real registered gear
- **THEN** evidence distinguishes committed fresh reload from in-memory changes and records every neutral weapon exception

### Requirement: Combined ratio weapon and tactical calibration covers both combat sides
Calibration SHALL exercise real player/enemy damage with basic attacks, representative physical skills, magical controls, bypass, conditional multipliers, max-HP riders, authored scales and multi-strike. It SHALL record the combined coefficient, critical, weapon and strike-count budget and compare estimator results with settlement.

#### Scenario: Complete factor matrix
- **WHEN** a fixed-seed calibration matrix runs
- **THEN** each required factor combination and control has observed outcomes rather than registry-echo assertions, including unchanged enemy defensive/evasive skills

#### Scenario: Evidence-supported tuning
- **WHEN** combined results identify an overbudget authored magnitude
- **THEN** only the implicated authored values are changed with before/after evidence; no unsupported global weakening or new progression bonus is introduced
