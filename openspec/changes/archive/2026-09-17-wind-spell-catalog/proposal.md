## Why

The wind tree (`docs/lore/skill-trees/wind.md`) is a 13-node two-root lineage — 機動路線 (疾風術→疾風連步→神速殘影→神速領域) and 破壞路線 (風刃術→龍捲風刃 branching into 暴風領域→天譴風暴→蒼穹暴風 and 疾風刃舞→天穹裂斬→真空斬滅, converging on the two-parent 神格 canopy 天穹神格) — while the registry still ships the dev-era ten-key set with no prerequisites, no coefficients, and a display-only `bounds`-bounds agility illusion (`wind_haste`/`wind_haste_domain`). The wave's two behavior changes land the generic vocabularies this data rides: the positional marker primitive (`displaced-knockback` — the 擊退 nodes' home) and the unconditional multi-strike policy (`unconditional-multi-strike` — gale_dance_strike's 「連續兩次判定」). Everything else the lore prices is already shipped at master: the `combat_modifiers.yaml` `buff_active` timed-ladder shape (earth `earth_hardened_skin`→`{defense: 3}` precedent — the ad084c9 insight says `bounds` is display/cap vocabulary with NO settlement consumer, so the 敏捷 +3/+5/+8/+12 ladder authors as modifier rules over empty-modifier mount buffs), `bypass_defense` execution, `max_hp_fraction` devastation, the n-ary DAG + two-parent capstone validator, and the freeform mastery ladder.
