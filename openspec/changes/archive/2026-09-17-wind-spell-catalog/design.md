### D2 — Knockback mounts as effect components, not reaction rules

The lore reads 「附加擊退效果」 — knocked back **by the strike itself**. The shipped `hp_loss` outcome dispatch carries `source_tier` but deliberately no `source_skill` (`combat.py:475`), so `event: hp_loss` + `event_source_skill:` gating cannot exist today, and `physical_hit` would mis-target wind's magic-school strikes. The `buff_apply` effect component is the shipped same-settlement mount (`action.py:572+`, audience-filtered, tier-resolved) and is exactly how `lava_burst` mounts its terrain marker in the archived fire precedent — positional knockback is the direct positional-axis analog. Consequences (all shipped semantics, zero new code): the displaced mount lands on every selected enemy whose damage leg lands (per-component independent settlement); the displaced-knockback ground-sweep-on-mount fires (blown off lava/fissure — the deterministic cross-primitive synergy); defeated/fled/KO recipients refuse the mount; area knockback never touches allies (ALLY/ENEMIES audience). Three distinct buff keys (`displaced` / `displaced_tempest` / `displaced_apotheosis`) at 30/60/90 s because row-level duration is the shipped mechanism (the earth `earth_fissure`/`_quake`/`_apex` trio precedent) — no tier-varying-duration mechanic exists or is invented.

### D3 — The accuracy/evasion two-pole rungs ride the two shipped to-hit terms

`_to_hit` (`combat.py:220-240`) reads exactly two authored-relevant bundle terms: attacker `accuracy` (flat) and both sides' `adjusted_agility` (percent then flat, floored at 0). The wind poles author one rule each on that vocabulary: `afterimage_step` pays 攻擊被命中率下降 as `{agility_flat: 8, accuracy: -10}` on its own mount (self-pole: faster body, less precise strike — the §4.2 same-axis bipolar legality; -10 derived from the shipped `fear_agility_and_accuracy_penalty` accuracy rung, the only accuracy number in the table, D4); `haste_domain` 提升迴避 lands on `{agility_flat: 12}` alone — on the DEFENDER side that term IS evasion (it raises the hit threshold), so the ally-domain buff raises every ally's dodge for free through the same rule; a separate `{agility: "+%"}` row would double-apply through `adjusted_agility` and invent a second representation of one clause. Rejected: a distinct `evasion` bundle key (no consumer exists; inventing one is new behavior code — forbidden by Context).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Sibling dependency**: this change authors data on vocabularies shipped BY THE WAVE (first two batch rows) — first OpenSpec wave where the catalog depends on unarchived siblings; mitigated by the declared queue + `depends-on:` lines; if a sibling's merge revises a design detail (row key, gate timing), the D1/D2 tables are the reconciliation point, and the catalog's tasks re-read the merged spec before authoring.

- **Three main-spec illustrative premises go stale** (`skill-registry` 風 table row, the display census example if any, the `test_buffs` wind pair) — all recorded as main-sync items (D6/D9), no requirement logic touched.
