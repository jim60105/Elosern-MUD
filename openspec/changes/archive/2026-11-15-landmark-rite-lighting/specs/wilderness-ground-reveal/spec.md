# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Permanent reveal is a bounded read projection
A wilderness cell SHALL reveal from valid lit ownership or a stored visit disc, while hidden location identity requires actual visitation and read queries write nothing. Lit ownership SHALL be supplied to the projection by its caller from the completed-rite record; the visit record SHALL derive no lighting.

#### Scenario: Union and lit shortcut
- **WHEN** synthetic discs span chunk/query edges and another district's rite is completed
- **THEN** reveal equals independent disc/ownership results using only expanded intersecting chunks

#### Scenario: Hidden and corrupt history
- **WHEN** a hidden cell reveals or an observation chunk is malformed
- **THEN** terrain may reveal without location identity and corruption retains bytes while using only trusted lit knowledge

#### Scenario: A visited landmark alone
- **WHEN** a landmark node is visited and no rite is completed there
- **THEN** only the visit's own disc reveals and the rest of that district stays hidden
