# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Encounter packages expose distinct escort commissions and final-result observation
Published packages SHALL permit explicit distinct commission and final-result group references without autoacceptance. Both independent encounter orders SHALL retain exact role identities. Relevant activation SHALL settle before final movement-result observation under the same source. Upstream actor availability/retention and participant-limited phase authority SHALL remain.

#### Scenario: Both encounter orders preserve distinct offers
- **WHEN** the same synthetic package's independent issuer encounters activate in each order
- **THEN** each role retains its exact binding, separate authorized offers remain available until actual final-result resolution, and neither encounter accepts a quest

#### Scenario: Activation precedes movement result evaluation
- **WHEN** movement makes a meeting encounter eligible and materializes the exact meeting role
- **THEN** its activation settles before final escort predicate observation using the same source, permitting one actual result without two stage transitions

#### Scenario: Complete encounter packages expose distinct escort commissions and final-result observation contract
- **WHEN** the operation is exercised through its deterministic owner with valid and rejecting inputs
- **THEN** The upstream published package contract SHALL permit explicitly declared multiple distinct commission references with validated exclusive-group final-result references. Publication SHALL never autoaccept; encounter activation SHALL retain either independent role-first order and exact persistent actors. Relevant activation SHALL occur before final movement-result escort observation under the same authoritative source identity. An activated role SHALL retain upstream availability/retention rules; missing/dead/departed actors SHALL never be cloned or teleported as escort repair. Participant result knowledge and acknowledgment/closure phases SHALL use the upstream authorized phase transition operation.
