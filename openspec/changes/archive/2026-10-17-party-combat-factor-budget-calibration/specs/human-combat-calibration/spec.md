# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Combined ratio weapon and tactical calibration covers both combat sides
Calibration SHALL exercise real player/enemy damage with basic attacks, representative physical skills, magical controls, bypass, conditional multipliers, max-HP riders, authored scales and multi-strike. It SHALL record the combined coefficient, critical, weapon and strike-count budget and compare estimator results with settlement.

#### Scenario: Complete factor matrix
- **WHEN** a fixed-seed calibration matrix runs
- **THEN** each required factor combination and control has observed outcomes rather than registry-echo assertions, including unchanged enemy defensive/evasive skills

#### Scenario: Evidence-supported tuning
- **WHEN** combined results identify an overbudget authored magnitude
- **THEN** only the implicated authored values are changed with before/after evidence; no unsupported global weakening or new progression bonus is introduced
