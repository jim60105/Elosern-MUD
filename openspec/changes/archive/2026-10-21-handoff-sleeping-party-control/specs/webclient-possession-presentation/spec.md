# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Browser and text expose the same non-acting handoff choices
Authenticated browser and text clients SHALL expose lawful sleep-driven handoff, cancel, status and recovery waiting even when the controlled body cannot act. Candidate fatigue and return/possess meaning SHALL be textual. Existing focus/selection conventions and epoch stale-response protection SHALL apply without a new visual redesign.

#### Scenario: Keyboard and stale response
- **WHEN** a keyboard user opens handoff and confirms after its candidate or presentation epoch changed
- **THEN** the shared core rejects stale selection, focus remains usable, and an old actor response cannot overwrite the new selection

#### Scenario: No model dependency
- **WHEN** model services are offline when the body sleeps and there is no awake candidate
- **THEN** status and explicit safe recovery wait work on both clients without generation; present hostiles still block skipping
