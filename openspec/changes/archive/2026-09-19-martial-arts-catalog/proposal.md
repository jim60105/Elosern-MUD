## Why

`docs/lore/skill-trees/martial-arts.md` is the node-data authority for a 14-node, two-tree martial lineage — 劍術 (見習劍術 root branching at 順勢連斬 into a single-target execution chain and an area destruction chain, converging on the two-parent 大劍豪 canopy) and 影流刀術 (影斬 root branching at 幻影連斬 into 雙刃旋舞 and 暗影獵殺, converging on the two-parent 暗影終劍 canopy) — while the registry still ships the dev-era five-key 武技 set (`basic_attack`, `dual_wield_style`, `dual_blade_mastery`, `light_sword_style`, `shadow_slash`, plus `flee`) with no prerequisites, no coefficients, no SP ladder and no riders. Every mechanic the lore prices is already shipped at master: the n-ary prerequisite DAG with two-parent capstones and derived tip caps, unconditional multi-strike (`extra_strikes` 1 and 2), `bypass_defense` execution, the `max_hp_fraction` devastation rung, `buff_apply`/`self_buff_apply` mounts over `buff_active` modifier rules, DoT `rate` rows, and `skill_owned` cast conditions. The one gap — a damage effect with no element, which the plain-steel 劍術 route needs — is landed by the predecessor change `elementless-damage-effect`.

## What Changes

- **Batch declaration.** This change lands SECOND and last of the martial wave, after `elementless-damage-effect` merges, per `## Batch` below with a machine-readable `depends-on:` line.
- Author the 14-node two-tree lineage into `SKILL_REGISTRY` per the node table AS-IS (keys, labels, targets, SP costs, prerequisites, coefficients, riders). The nine 劍術 nodes are elementless (`element=None`, `damage:none:physical` — the predecessor's token); the five 影流 nodes keep `dark`, which is lore-true for a 基亞蘭 shadow art. Every new ACTIVE node declares `usable_out_of_combat=True` (the frozen `USABLE_OUT_OF_COMBAT_FALSE` inventory stays exactly `{"flee"}`).
- `shadow_slash` is re-authored in place as the 影流 root (its shipped SP 18 and `dark` element are already the authored values; it gains the coefficient and its position in the graph). `dual_blade_mastery` retires without an alias: it is the same move as the tree's `dual_blade_waltz` (雙刃旋舞, SINGLE, SP 30, dark physical) under a stale key whose `_mastery` suffix is this project's element-mastery-passive naming convention. `basic_attack`, `light_sword_style`, `dual_wield_style` and `flee` are untouched by this change (the predecessor converts `basic_attack` to the elementless form as part of landing the token; this change does not re-touch that row).
- Author the three rider rows the lore prices — 斷筋 (`martial_hamstring`, 60 s, empty-modifier mount), 暗影創傷 (`shadow_wound`, 60 s DoT at the shipped `rate` band), 劍豪之境 (`sword_saint_domain`, 60 s, empty-modifier mount) — plus their two `buff_active` modifier rules (`{agility_flat: -5}`, `{atk_phys: 12}`) and the five matching `status_display.yaml` census rows (three buff keys, two rule IDs; that table fails closed on drift over both vocabularies).
- Gate the 影流 nodes above the root on the 架式 the lore requires: `CastCondition(ACTOR, {"skill_owned": "dual_wield_style"})` on `phantom_dance` and everything above it. The root `shadow_slash` stays ungated — the lore's 流派開放性 note has outsiders 「從影斬一節起步」, and gating a shipped root would silently disarm it for existing owners.
- Retire the echo requirement `skill-registry::dual_blade_mastery exists as a higher-tier sibling to dual_wield_style` (REMOVED) and replace it with a behavioral martial-progression contract (ADDED) proved by synthetic compositions. Update the shipped-content category census key set (`world/skills/tests/test_skill_registry.py`, an existing data-contract test) and `world/lore/player_presets.py`'s 悠花 row to the new key. **No new data-contract test is added by this change** — no key-set, node-table, cost, cap or tier echo, and no lineage-edge catalog test.
