# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mental intervals compose with the single clock writer
Each accepted noncombat advance SHALL carry validated per-member mental activity and policy, with a separate present-party mental scope when ordinary resource scope is narrower. Physiology and projections SHALL commit with the tick through the existing writer and rollback snapshots, without changing the fixed order of unrelated stages.

#### Scenario: Rite scope separation
- **WHEN** a time-bearing rite settles one participant while its party is present
- **THEN** the same tick accounts for all present mental intervals exactly once while unrelated rite/gauge/quest scope stays unchanged

#### Scenario: Fault after later event
- **WHEN** a registered boundary writer fails after mental writes
- **THEN** tick, all mental-scope members, buffs, bookings and touched in-process caches restore, including members outside ordinary resource scope
