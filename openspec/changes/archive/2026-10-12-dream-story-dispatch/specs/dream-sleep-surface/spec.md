# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Confirmed dream handoff returns to playable waking surfaces

Browser and text confirmation SHALL return immediately to waking play with a truthful receipt distinct from arrangement. Preparation, waiting, arrangement and failure SHALL use ordinary game output without a new story panel. Dispatch, reconnect and awakening SHALL preserve the original sleep settlement and SHALL NOT add game time. Existing deterministic gameplay SHALL remain available while generation waits or fails.

#### Scenario: Confirmation is a receipt
- **WHEN** a player confirms a valid direction on either surface
- **THEN** the dream closes, normal gameplay is available before generation finishes and feedback claims receipt rather than an existing NPC or completed quest

#### Scenario: Waiting while offline mechanics run
- **WHEN** required model services are disabled after confirmation
- **THEN** the player can move and use deterministic quests while work truthfully waits, without extra sleep settlement or generation time charge
