# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Story participation does not disclose private dispatch planning

Creative discussion, job status, future plans and private director snapshots SHALL NOT become NPC memories. Materialization SHALL initialize only role-permitted background knowledge with provenance through narrative owners. Conversation, information transfer, reunion and closure SHALL project later knowledge through existing APIs and upstream transition_story_dialogue. Adding a live role to a story SHALL NOT grant the owner's draft or all planning facts.

#### Scenario: Newly materialized girl knows only permitted background
- **WHEN** the girl activates from a package whose director context contains hidden future mother plans
- **THEN** scripted and free-form context contains only her permitted provenance-bearing knowledge, excluding private discussion, work status and undisclosed future facts

#### Scenario: Real reunion teaches actual participants
- **WHEN** committed conversation/information transfer or reunion teaches a participant a fact
- **THEN** subsequent everyday dialogue can recall that permitted experience while an uninformed remote actor receives no knowledge from global outcome state alone

#### Scenario: Failed generation changes no NPC memory
- **WHEN** a failed or stale work callback returns readable story/dialogue prose
- **THEN** it appends no obsolete dialogue exchange or NPC memory and changes no committed world history
