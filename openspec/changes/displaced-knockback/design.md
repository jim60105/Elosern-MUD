## Context

Delivers the generic **out-of-position (displaced) marker** primitive the wind tree prices on three nodes — 暴風領域「附加擊退效果」、蒼穹暴風「附加擊退控制」、天穹神格「附加擊退控制，持續 90 秒」(`docs/lore/skill-trees/wind.md`; constitution §4.2 wind row: 「擊退＝位置極性的合法自用」). Per the user-ratified naming requirement the primitive is element-agnostic mechanic vocabulary — a future charge, shockwave, or grapple-break rides the same fact; the wind rows (`displaced` buff keys, durations, the knockback `buff_apply` mounts, the three consumer skills) belong to `wind-spell-catalog`. Pre-audited engine facts (verified at master 9ca95ba):

- **No position primitive exists.** In-combat position is binary: the battlefield roster tracks `fled`/`knocked_out` sets only (`world/rules/combat_session.py`); the four targeting validators are presence/alive/range/faction (`world/rules/targeting.py`), and range is a context protocol answer, not per-strike-class. The `terrain-marker` wave proved the modeling answer for the *ground* axis; this change is its sibling on the *position* axis.
- **The buff layer already models the shape**: `MARKER_VOCABULARY = frozenset({"ground"})` is a validated top-level definition clause (`world/rules/buffs.py:39,216-223`), `remove_ground_markers` (`buffs.py:779`) is the scoped sweep helper already wired at the three session-settlement writes (`combat_session.py:605,1421-1444`), and `entity_active_buffs` already answers 「holds the key」 for `combat_modifiers`, `spell_conditions` and the `buff:` predicate seam.
- **The strike-class distinction is already derivable from shipped vocabulary**: `_parse_damage_effect` (`world/rules/combat.py:269-280`) splits `damage:<element>:<school>` into school ∈ {physical, magic}; `TargetRequirement.spec` (`world/rules/targeting.py`) says SINGLE vs AREA. A 「單體物理近身攻擊」 is exactly `spec is SINGLE` ∧ every damage effect is school `physical` ∧ at least one damage effect exists (the shipped `basic_attack` row — `effects=["damage:fire:physical"]`, `TargetSpec.SINGLE` — is the canonical STRIKE; a mage spell like `fire_ball` is SINGLE+magic and is NOT a strike). No new field is needed to classify.
- **Step 4a already owns state-dependent target rejection**: `evaluate_interaction_policy` (`world/rules/spell_conditions.py`) raises `RejectedAction(CAST_CONDITION_UNMET)` for fled/uncapable targets mid-workflow on contact-ritual skills (the ordinary-skill precedent for state-dependent rejection is its sibling `evaluate_cast_conditions`, dispatched from the same seam) (`action.py:_step4a_spell_conditions`, lines 417-428, after `_step3_targeting`/`_step4_capability`) — the precedent shape for a skill-shape-dependent gate that the four-validator `TargetRequirement` deliberately cannot model.
- **Enemy selection is two deterministic filters today**: `_basic_attack_request` (`combat_session.py:488-517`) picks the lowest-HP living enemy from roster minus fled/knocked-out; `monster_behaviour._choose_target` (`monster_behaviour.py:219`) picks from a caller-built enemy list. Both feed `ActionRequest`s that the action workflow revalidates — a gate that only lives at resolution would strand scripted actions into guaranteed rejections.

User-ratified givens (supervisor-ratified design, NOT reopened): knockback == a generic element-agnostic `displaced` marker debuff mounted on the individual; holding == out of position == melee-unreachable BOTH ways; area and magic fully reachable; holder keeps full agency (NOT ice's stillness ladder, NOT thunder's turn denial — index.md §4.2/§4.4 sovereignty); self-return clears on the holder's own single-target strike action; knockback sweeps the holder's ground markers; never mounts on fled/knocked_out/defeated entities and extinguishes on those transitions. **No second rules engine, no element-key branches in generic code.** **NON-GOAL: 遊戲資料契約** — verification is behavior contracts on synthetic buffs/skills/state transitions only; this change ships zero live rows (inert-but-valid vocabulary, the `terrain-marker`/`caster_share` precedent). **Clean cut, zero users**: no migrations, no aliases.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** One declarative `positional` value on the closed marker vocabulary; holding-the-marker == out-of-position as the canonical fact; the bidirectional single-target-physical-strike gate over shipped school/target classification; the self-return clear on the holder's own strike; ground-marker sweep-on-mount as the deterministic cross-primitive synergy; battlefield-exit (fled/knocked_out/defeated/session-end) extinguishment scoped to positional rows; selection-filter hygiene for the deterministic enemy pickers; behavior-test-only proof; every piece generic.

**Non-Goals:** No wind registry rows, no `displaced` buff row, no knockback mount data (`wind-spell-catalog` authors all data); no `combat_modifiers.yaml` row for the marker (its whole meaning is the reachability gate, not a numeric adjustment — the ad084c9 insight: bounds is display/cap vocabulary with no settlement consumer; a modifier row would be a second, dead representation); no action-lock, turn denial, or movement freeze (explicitly ice's/thunder's verbs — the holder casts, moves, uses items, acts); no room/tile position axis; no fifth targeting validator (the `TargetRequirement` shape model stays untouched — `targeting-validation` requirements bit-identical); no data-contract tests; no `world/rules/combat.py` edit (the gate is targeting/condition-layer, not the damage loop).

## Decisions

### D1 — Carrier: `marker: positional`, the second closed value of the shipped marker clause

| Axis | extend MARKER_VOCABULARY (chosen) | new top-level clause (rejected) | modifiers key (rejected) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar | `marker` already means 「holding the row IS a positional/state fact about the holder」 — ground is the same primitive family on a different axis; the vocabulary is one closed enum, extending it names the family instead of fragmenting it | a sibling clause (e.g. `position:`) splits one concept across two fields and doubles the validation surface | `modifiers` stays the closed effect table (`rate`/`bounds`/`divert`); identity belongs beside `duration`/`polarity`, not inside effects |
| Lifecycle | inherits the D2-of-terrain-marker exit machinery by consulting `defn.marker == "positional"` — one sweep family, two scoped selectors | new removal machinery branch | — |
| Fact read | `entity_active_buffs` answers 「holds a positional row」 with zero new query surface | — | — |

Clause shape: `marker: positional` on the same top-level field; `MARKER_VOCABULARY` grows to `frozenset({"ground", "positional"})`; malformed values keep failing the load naming the offending key. A positional row may carry `rate`/`bounds`/`modifiers` compositionally (the sweep and gate consult the clause only). `BuffDefinition` needs no new field — `marker: str | None` already carries it. The default duration for lore-silent knockback rows (design note for the catalog: §3.3 大師 30 s / 主宰 60 s / 神格 90 s bands; the node table prices 天穹神格 at 90 s explicitly and leaves the other two rows lore-silent — the catalog authors ~20–30 s ≈ two rounds for `storm_domain` per §3.3; this change only guarantees any authored world-second duration behaves identically).

### D2 — The reachability gate: step-4a spell-conditions, both directions, STRIKE-class only

The gate is a state-dependent, **skill-shape-dependent** rejection — precisely what `evaluate_interaction_policy` already does for fled/uncapable targets — so it lives in the step-4a seam (`spell_conditions.evaluate_cast_conditions` extended, or a sibling function called from `_step4a_spell_conditions`; implementation may choose, the contract is the position: after target resolution, before effect staging):

1. **Classification (generic, no element keys):** an action is a STRIKE iff `requirement_for(skill).spec is TargetSpec.SINGLE` and the skill declares ≥1 `damage:*:physical` effect and no `damage:*:magic` effect. (Magic-school SINGLE spells are never strikes; area spells are never single-target; the shipped classification readers — `_parse_damage_effect`, `TargetSpec` — are reused, no new registry field.)
2. **Direction holder→other:** while the marker is live, the holder cannot *select* others for single-target physical strikes — the holder→other pairing is unreachable (the ratified both-ways gate). The single exemption is the ratified self-return seam (D4): the holder's own single-target STRIKE action is the act of climbing back — the marker clears immediately before that action resolves and the strike lands as the one priced re-entry. Every other path stays gated while the marker is live: a second same-window strike attempt, magic/area/item/movement never open or close the pairing.
3. **Direction other→holder:** a STRIKE whose selected target holds a live positional instance rejects with the same reason naming the target.
4. **Exemptions (positive by construction, not branch-by-exception):** AREA skills, magic-school SINGLE skills, NONE/SELF skills, items, movement, and every capability path are untouched — the gate consults the STRIKE classification and the marker clause only. No `actions_per_turn`, no `blocks_action`, no `BLOCKING_BUFF_KEYS` entry: the holder's own turn, casting, and movement are sovereign (§4.4).
5. **Why not a fifth validator:** `_VALIDATORS` is requirement-pinned as exactly four ordered definition-shape-independent checks (`targeting-validation`), shared verbatim by preview and revalidation; the displaced gate depends on the *acting skill's* effect composition, so validator placement would force `TargetRequirement` to carry effect-shape knowledge it deliberately omits and would wrongly gate AREA/magic expansion. Step 4a is where skill- and state-dependent rejection already lives.

`action_preview` paths that route through the same step-4a condition functions inherit the rejection verbatim; paths that don't call step 4a today (pure preview) keep their read-only behavior — the resolution gate remains the single authority (the same posture `terrain-marker` took for synergy reads).

### D3 — Mount path: shipped reaction vocabulary + boundary refuse semantics

The wind data mounts displaced as **direct `buff_apply:` effect components** on the consumer skills (the fire-capstone `buff_apply:fire_scorch` precedent shape) riding the shipped effect handler into the public `apply_buff` entry — `state_reactions.yaml` needs ZERO change and the catalog authors ZERO reaction rows (the catalog's D2 owns the data decision). A reaction-rule mount was audited and is IMPOSSIBLE for the wind nodes at master 9ca95ba: the `hp_loss` dispatch carries `source_tier` but NOT `source_skill` (`world/rules/combat.py:475-477`; same shape at `action.py:1820`), and the `physical_hit` dispatch carries `source_skill` but fires only for physical-school damage — so an `event: hp_loss` + `event_source_skill:` gate can never express 「wind storm knocks back」. The component mount is also simpler and settlement-independent exactly like the fire precedent (the effect components land on selected targets whether or not the damage leg lands; the boundary refuse below handles the killed-by-damage case cleanly). Durations are row-level (three node-priced knockbacks author as three distinct positional rows at 30/60/90 s exactly like the shipped `earth_fissure`/`earth_fissure_quake`/`earth_fissure_apex` trio — no tier-varying duration mechanism is needed or invented; see D7). One new mount-path semantic closes generically here:

- **Boundary refuse:** the `apply_buff` write path refuses mounting a positional row on an entity that is defeated (hp ≤ 0), or is recorded fled/knocked-out — the fled/knocked-out consult resolves the active battlefield's knocked-out/fled sets for effect-component recipients (the session record exists only on the player-side actor, `combat_session.py:4,576-577`; entity-side `read_session` lookup is the actor-side fallback), **and** — via D5's sweep symmetry — a positional row never survives those transitions either way (the exit sweep fires at the same three persisted writes plus defeat settlement). The refuse is clause-scoped (consults `defn.marker == "positional"`), never a key branch; `ground` rows keep their exact mounted-anyway-then-swept behavior bit-identical.

### D4 — Self-return: the holder's own strike IS the return (the ratified rule, sequenced)

The supervisor-ratified sentence is 「when the holder resolves its own single-target strike action, the displaced marker clears BEFORE the action resolves (climbing back into position)」. The sequencing that makes it consistent with the both-ways gate — fixed order inside step 4a for STRIKE-class actions: (1) **target-side gate first**: if the selected target holds a live positional instance, the action rejects with the condition-unmet reason and the ACTOR's marker is NOT consumed (a failed climb-back toward an unreachable target leaves the climber out of position — no double punishment); (2) **self-return clear**: a STRIKE-class action that passed (1) whose **actor** holds a live positional instance clears the actor's own live positional instances immediately before the action resolves (staged in the same transaction/snapshot as the rest of the workflow, so a failed settlement restores the marker); (3) the action proceeds normally — the strike lands. Consequences, all intended: the displaced state costs the holder exactly one successful climb-back action (its next melee strike against a reachable target spends the marker instead of being blocked — 「climbing back into position」 as the position polarity's action cost); the holder cannot stay displaced while striking repeatedly — after the first landing strike it is back in position; every non-strike action (magic, area, item, movement, turn passage) NEVER clears the marker; a second same-window strike attempt after the clear is ordinary (the holder is in position again). Self-return only ever clears the *actor's own* marker. `remove_positional_markers(entity) -> int` mirrors `remove_ground_markers`' shape (scoped selector, zero damage side effects, returns the count).

Rejected: rejecting the holder's own strike and clearing on expiry only (makes the strike a guaranteed-rejection dead end, contradicts the ratified self-return rule, and prices 擊退 as thunder's turn denial — §4.4 forbidden); clearing at the holder's NEXT turn start via upkeep (a scheduler concept the clock doesn't model, and it would let a displaced holder freely melee at will until the first turn boundary); clearing on any action (erases the action cost and lets a displaced holder spell-cast its way back invisibly).

### D5 — Ground sweep on mount + symmetric exit sweeps (the cross-primitive synergy)

Applying a positional row SHALL first remove the recipient's live `ground` marker instances (blown off the fissure/lava), then mount. Ordering is deterministic and observable: sweep count/log line (`combat_marker_swept`-family observability event via the `world.observability` facade) rides inside the shipped `apply_buff` write path, scoped strictly to `defn.marker == "ground"` instances; non-marker buffs untouched; the positional mount itself is a normal refresh-stacking apply. The symmetric exit rule extends the three existing `remove_ground_markers` call sites (`combat_session.py` fled/knocked_out/session-end) to positional rows too — one selector widening (`defn.marker in {"ground","positional"}`… implemented as the two scoped helpers so each requirement sentence stays separately observable). Defeat (hp ≤ 0 outside knockout protection) already sweeps at the same persisted transitions; the positional row adds no new call site.

### D6 — Selection-filter hygiene: the deterministic pickers skip displaced candidates

Three deterministic single-target pickers exist today and all three SHALL exclude positional-marker holders from their STRIKE-class candidate sets: `_basic_attack_request` (`combat_session.py:488`) and `default_attack_policy` (`combat.py:754` — delegated non-Monster attackers) are inherently strike paths (basic/melee attack requests), so the exclusion applies unconditionally there; `monster_behaviour`'s `_living_enemies`→`_choose_target` path chooses a TARGET before a skill, so the state-only hygiene rule is: exclude displaced candidates when the acting monster's owned SINGLE-target damage kit is exclusively strike-class (or empty), while a monster owning magic-school SINGLE damage (`tactical_caster` archetypes) MAY keep the full candidate set for its magic kit with the step-4a gate authoritative for any strike it happens to pick (implementation may alternatively order the exclusion after skill choice — the contract is the requirement's, the ordering is the implementer's; see the amended `monster-action-policy` delta). The AREA `all-enemies` shorthand path keeps displaced candidates — area reachability is unaffected. Each falls back to no-target/no-action when no candidate survives the class-scoped exclusion (the round proceeds with that entity skipping its attack exactly like the fled-empty case — deterministic, no dice). Rationale: the resolution gate makes a displaced-enemy strike a guaranteed `CAST_CONDITION_UNMET`; today's fled filter proves the selection layer is the right home for shape-independent unreachability. The filters are convenience, never permission: resolution-time rejection stays the single authority, and out-of-combat/magic paths are untouched (monsters keep their own casting policy unchanged — the filter applies only to strike-class selection). The `monster-action-policy` requirement 「Target selection differs by archetype…」 is MODIFIED as the strict superset adding the displaced exclusion to the candidate set (the fixed-seed golden tests stay deterministic: the filter is state-only, dice-free).

### D7 — Duration-band decision for the lore-silent rows (guidance to `wind-spell-catalog`)

The node table prices only 天穹神格's knockback (90 s). For `storm_domain`/`sky_tempest` the design decision belongs to the catalog's data; this design ratifies the derivation: §3.3 control-duration bands (大師 30 s, 主宰 60 s) with the 90 s row anchored by the node table — suggested authored values 30 s / 60 s / 90 s, i.e. the two lore-silent rows ride ~20–30 s ≈ two rounds upward from the band floor, pinned by the band table, not invented. This change's contract: ANY authored world-second duration ticks/refreshes/expires identically (verified synthetically at arbitrary durations).
