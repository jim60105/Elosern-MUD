### D4 — thunder_gods_haste's extra action

`lightning_extra_action` mount re-homed 60→12 s, empty modifiers (the bounds illusion dies); settlement rule `thunder_gods_haste_grants_action` (`buff_active: lightning_extra_action` → `actions_per_turn: 1`), merged onto the implicit baseline 1 → 2 actions. Round-clock timing, stated honestly: the loop reads the bundle once at the combatant's slot, and the per-round upkeep ticks buffs AFTER the action phase — so a 6 s mount cast during a round is expired at that round's upkeep and never seen by the next round's read. The authored duration is therefore 12 s (two round windows): cast in round R, the mount survives R's upkeep, is live at the holder's slot in round R+1 (the 「本回合額外一次行動機會」 lands on the round the loop next provisions the holder), and expires at R+1's upkeep unused-or-not. The alternative — re-reading the bundle at a second slot inside the same round — was rejected (it would fork the bundle read into two authorities per combatant per round and change the shipped skip-gate semantics); the 12 s row is the honest data-side answer inside shipped loop mechanics.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **`paralysis` 30→20 s is a live cross-consumer change:** dev-era `paralyzing_bolt` is the only shipped applier; quests/presets/spawns re-checked for `paralysis` references (the ice-wave census pattern). The 10 s shortening is lightning pricing its own rung per the node table.
- **The flicker-both-stuns merge rides the maximum-chance fold** — order-independent by construction (turn-order-control's matched-bundle reading), recorded as the wave's only co-existing chance pair; alternative (single merged rule requiring both stun markers) is inexpressible — `when` ANDs one `buff_active` key per rule.
- **60 s markers outlive one round** by design: retreat/advance ops are no-ops for already-acted keys, so a still-live marker on a key that already relocated has no further effect that round; next round the sequence re-rolls and a stale marker can still relocate a not-yet-acted holder (refresh-while-mounted keeps re-applying — 「微階」 sustained as long as the attacker keeps striking the ward and the round hasn't turned over for them; bounded by the 60 s row — the fire_scorching_armor mount-row duration precedent (mount/detection rows carry their own durations; §3.3 bands price the CONTROL rungs: the 麻痺 rows, the chance decision itself is per-round stateless)).
- **`flicker_advance` 12 s vs 60 s mount split:** the caster's own advance is the two-window grant (the lore line prices the rewrite, not a standing aura — D4's timing analysis); the flicker detection mount is 60 s — two rows, honest two-effect composition.
