# Proposal

## Why

The road generator joins capitals, cities, ports and towns only. The village and hamlet tiers design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-village-hamlet-tiers-design.md` §6) requires every village to be reached by one spur road, without moving any existing road, and requires that a hamlet can stand on a cell that already carries a road.

## What Changes

- The generator gains a second stage. Stage one is the current algorithm with the current nodes. Stage two processes every `village` location in identifier order and routes one spur from the village's cell to the node that is cheapest to walk to. Candidate nodes are every stage-one node and every village already processed. A spur may reuse road cells at the configured reuse factor.
- A village is a spur endpoint by its kind and cell alone. It needs no settlement document.
- The cells of `village` and `hamlet` locations stay open to roads: their footprints and gateways are not obstacles for the road generator. Stage one is therefore independent of every village and hamlet.
- The artifact marks a spur road with the village it belongs to; a road without the mark is a trunk. With no village in the input, the artifact is byte-for-byte what the generator writes today.
- Generation fails, naming the village, when a village cell is not on the cost field or reaches no node.
- The preview draws spurs in a second colour.
- Documentation of the spur stage in `docs/development/road-network.md`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `road-network-generation`: stage-one nodes exclude spur kinds; village and hamlet cells are open; a spur stage is added; stage one is independent of villages and hamlets; generation refuses an unreachable village.
- `road-network-artifact`: a road may carry a spur owner, and an artifact with no spur keeps the current bytes.
