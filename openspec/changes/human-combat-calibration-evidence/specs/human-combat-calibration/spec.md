## Purpose

Define record conditional human calibration and verify real gear/restrictions in bounded resolver probes as observable deterministic behavior with explicit rejection and acceptance boundaries.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Human calibration uses real resolver gear restrictions and bounded outcome evidence
Human-only calibration SHALL preserve approved section 8 tables/conditions as conditional historical projected evidence and record new real gear/restriction/persistent-host smoke separately. Real Evennia objects, usable lineage/proficiency, actual registered items/accessories, ActionResolver, initiative, costs/upkeep and run_round SHALL be exercised with full pools, simulation markers and 200-round bound. No combat math duplication, skill coefficient tuning, elf/beastfolk target or new species SHALL be introduced.

#### Scenario: Real restrictions
- **WHEN** unchanged persistent host wears actual exam kit
- **THEN** all restriction consumers match and immutable normal records survive terminal restoration

#### Scenario: Marker discipline
- **WHEN** probe resolves skills/rounds
- **THEN** no growth/unlock drift or ordinary kill reward occurs

### Requirement: Evidence separates defeat retreat support safety and unverified projections
Evidence SHALL record seeds/build/item/profile/backend/engine revision and distinct monster defeats, retreats, human losses, all-standing, unfinished, rejected actions and round medians. Seeds 0-3 SHALL bound new focused probes; original 0-15/0-7 tables SHALL retain their qualifications. A retreat SHALL NOT establish kill/loot/hunt credit; high/calamity probes SHALL remain unshipped and non-universal. A real-kit mismatch SHALL block acceptance until diagnosed in the owning change without silently altering approved values.

#### Scenario: Retreat
- **WHEN** monster behavior policy flees
- **THEN** encounter escape is reported separately from zero-HP defeat with no inferred kill credit

#### Scenario: Upper boundary
- **WHEN** S succeeds in a sampled high-upper matchup
- **THEN** report does not promise universal S victory or all-standing A-party safety
