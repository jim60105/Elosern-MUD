# Proposal

## Why

About 300 villages and hamlets will be placed by implementing agents over six content changes. The village and hamlet tiers design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-village-hamlet-tiers-design.md` §4 and §8.3) requires one tested validation step that checks the committed result in CI, because the placement script itself is a throwaway tool with no tests.

## What Changes

- A new command, `uv run --locked python -m tools.settlement_spacing`, checks the committed locations and road artifact and exits non-zero on any problem. It writes nothing.
- Minimum spacing, in straight-line kilometres between cell centres:
  - a hamlet is at least 15 km from every capital, city, town, port, village and other hamlet;
  - a village is at least 40 km from every capital, city, town, port and other village.
  - Elven villages and dungeons take no part. Pairs among capitals, cities, towns and ports are never checked.
- Road coverage: along every road of the artifact, consecutive settlements are at most 75 km apart by road length. A village or hamlet whose cell is a road cell counts as a settlement on every road through that cell.
- Road coverage applies only to gaps that lie wholly inside a completed area. The list of completed areas ships empty.
- An explicit exemption: a location may carry `spacing_exemption` with a non-blank reason; a marked village or hamlet is skipped by the two minimum-spacing rules.
- Placement identity rules for villages and hamlets: ID prefix, unique display name, a cell in a region that has a nation, lore nation equal to that nation, population in the kind's range, no cell in a road-forbidden region, and every village on a road cell.
- CI runs the command in the preflight job.
- Documentation of the spacing rules in the map authoring skill.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `settlement-spacing`: the validation of minimum spacing, road coverage and placement identity for villages and hamlets.

### Modified Capabilities

- `map-geographic-sources`: a location accepts an optional spacing exemption reason.
