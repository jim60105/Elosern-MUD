# Proposal

## Why

The village and hamlet tiers design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-village-hamlet-tiers-design.md` §7) gives a village and a hamlet a one-cell footprint whose entrances follow the roads that meet it, two when a road passes through. Today a wilderness mask with exactly one `#` always means a concealed point entry: the cell stays walkable wilderness, exactly one gate is allowed, and the gate is taken by standing on the cell itself. A one-cell settlement that a road passes through cannot be expressed. This change adds the missing declaration; it is a prerequisite the tiers design did not list.

## What Changes

- A wilderness entry gains an optional boolean field `single_cell_footprint`. When it is true and the mask has exactly one `#`, the cell is a physical footprint: it is an obstacle cell, it may carry several gates, and each gate is taken from the neighbouring approach cell, like any multi-cell footprint.
- When the field is absent or false, a one-`#` mask keeps the concealed point-entry behaviour unchanged.
- The field on a mask that does not have exactly one `#` is a semantic diagnostic and has no effect.
- The terrain-fit lint treats a one-cell physical footprint as a footprint, not as a concealed entry.
- Documentation of the field in the settlement authoring reference.
- No shipped settlement uses the field in this change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `map-settlement-sources`: a one-cell mask may declare a physical footprint.
- `wilderness-gateway`: a one-cell physical footprint resolves its gateways at approach cells.
- `settlement-terrain-fit`: the lint's footprint and gate checks apply to every physical footprint, including a one-cell one.
