# Landmark Rites: gated district discovery and repeatable adult ritual scenes

**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Status:** Design approved by the user section by section on 2026-11-14. Written-spec review pending.
**Implementation status:** Not implemented by this document.
**Scope:** Replaces the instant district reveal at a landmark with an authored,
repeatable rite: the player (or one companion) chooses one of two or three
ritual stories, the deterministic core settles time and sexual state, and the
district lights only once that character has completed at least one rite there.
Covers the world rules, the authored content document, the presentation panel,
the text command, and the removal of the movement-time district flag.

## 1. Problem

A district is lit when the character's exact landmark node enters map
knowledge. Walking onto the landmark therefore flips a whole district to
explored in the same step, with no authored moment and nothing for the player
to do. The map has 120 districts; every one of them currently resolves the same
way.

The world already owns the pieces a richer reveal needs: authored scenes with a
manual browser player and a text-client emission, a female-coded sexual state
model (pleasure, arousal, wetness, shame, exposure, climax phase, experience),
a time-advance owner with a settlement loop, and an art pipeline keyed by scene
archetype. This design uses those pieces instead of adding a parallel system.

## 2. Goal

At a district landmark the player meets the landmark's guardian, is offered two
or three distinct ritual stories, performs one, and thereby earns the district's
discovery. Rituals remain available afterwards so the player can experience
every story at that landmark. Lighting is derived from that completed-rite
record, never from raw visits.

## 3. Scope and non-goals

**In scope**

- A new `landmark_rites` character record and its sole writer/reader.
- A new authored content document: ten ritual archetypes, their options and
  scenes, and the assignment of all 120 districts to one archetype.
- District lighting derived from that record; the reveal projection takes the
  lit set from the caller instead of deriving it from knowledge.
- One registered action, `explore.landmark_rite`, reachable from a webclient
  panel and from the text command `儀式`.
- A new `rite` presentation panel carrying the guardian, the options, and the
  most recently delivered scene.
- One new webclient card in the choices anchor. The existing shared authored
  scene player (`use-authored-scene.js` + `MessageWindow`) renders the
  delivery; no second renderer, pager, or typewriter is added.
- Removal of the movement-time `district_lit` interrupt and of the
  `landmark_reached` notice's `lit_district` field.
- Ten `scene:` art subjects, one per archetype, generated by the existing art
  pipeline.
- Player-facing command documentation and the observability catalog.

**Out of scope**

- Any LLM or image-generation dependency in the rite path. The flow is fully
  deterministic and playable offline; art is an asynchronous presentation
  asset that may be absent.
- The dreaming, defeat-aftermath, church, and quest systems. Rites award no
  merit, copper, experience, items, or quest progress.
- A global adult-content toggle. Declining a rite is per-visit and has no
  persisted consequence.
- Per-landmark bespoke scenes. Content is per archetype only.
- Migrating existing `map_knowledge` records.

## 4. Decisions

1. **Completion is an interaction.** A rite completes when the player selects
   one of the offered options in the `rite` panel or names one in the command.
   Reading the scene is presentation; it does not gate the commit.
2. **Participants.** The acting body is the controlled character or exactly one
   present party companion. The guardian is the landmark's presence: female,
   non-human, or a sexless force. No rite depicts a male or male-figured
   partner. Other companions are bystanders and receive no writes.
3. **Content scale.** Ten archetypes; each carries two options and gains a
   third when authored. Both perspectives (`self`, `watch`) are authored per
   option. Assignment is one archetype per district, all 120 districts
   assigned exactly once.
4. **Interaction form.** Panel options plus a cost. Rites are never free
   narration; each option advances world time and moves sexual state.
5. **Decline.** The card offers a dismissal that writes nothing, costs nothing,
   and may be repeated indefinitely. There is no persisted opt-out.
6. **Cost.** Sexual state on the acting body plus a few minutes of world time.
   No money, experience, items, or merit.
7. **Commit timing.** The server commits on the selection, atomically. A
   disconnect after the commit leaves the district lit and the record written;
   the scene is then presentation only.
8. **Repeatability.** Rites are always available at their landmark, lit or not.
   Repeating a rite re-settles the declared cost and updates the record;
   lighting is unchanged once earned.
9. **Lighting source.** A district is lit if and only if the character has at
   least one completed rite for it. Visited landmark nodes no longer light
   anything.
10. **City landmarks are in scope.** Bureaucratic exceptions are not needed:
    panel availability is decided by the character's canonical map node, so the
    one `settlement_node` landmark (王都中央廣場) is served by the same surface.
11. **Sexual framing.** Scenes may be penetrative or take any other form and
    may involve the clitoris, labia, urethra, anus, vagina, cervix, and uterus
    of the acting body. They never depict a male or male-figured partner.
12. **At least one climax.** Every option's settlement produces one mechanical
    orgasm. The prose shows that climax; the mechanism produces it through the
    canonical two-call pleasure sequence and the time advance's settlement.
13. **Art.** One `scene:` subject per archetype, generated lazily by the
    existing pipeline and presented through the existing scene descriptor
    chain. Missing art is a quiet ink frame.

## 5. Vocabulary and identity

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **archetype** | One authored ritual family keyed `[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,31}`, for example `spring`. It owns the guardian, its derived art subject, and two or three options. |
| **option** | One authored ritual story within an archetype, keyed `[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,31}` and unique within that archetype. It owns the cost, the scene perspectives, and the option label. |
| **rite landmark** | Any district whose authored landmark resolves to a canonical map node and whose district is assigned an archetype. All 120 districts are rite landmarks. |
| **acting body** | The character that performs the rite: the controlled character, or one present party companion named by the request. Only this body receives sexual-state writes. |
| **record owner** | The player character. Map knowledge and the rite record always belong to the player character, even when a companion performs. |
| **delivery** | The scene the server hands to the presentation layer for the last committed rite. |

The asset key of an archetype is a `scene:` art subject: `rite_<archetype>`
(for example `rite_spring`). It is registered as a `SceneArchetype` so
`scene_subject_for()` accepts it and the existing worker, gallery, and
presenter paths apply unchanged.

## 6. Authored content

### 6.1 Document

One document, `world/lore/rites/content/landmark_rites.yaml`, loaded by
`world/lore/rites/loader.py` through the same explicitly validated, snapshot
pattern as `world/lore/map_districts.py`. Shape dataclasses live in
`world/lore/rites/shape.py`; the rules layer never parses YAML.

Keeping the archetypes and the district assignment in one document lets one
validation pass prove completeness: every archetype referenced, every district
assigned exactly once, no unknown district, no unused archetype.

```yaml
version: 1
kind: landmark_rites
id: landmark_rites
archetypes:
  spring:
    guardian_name_zh: 泉之聲
    guardian_form_zh: 水面映出的聲音，只在有人俯身時開口。
    options:
      - key: drink
        label_zh: 俯身飲下
        seconds: 180
        shame: 1
        exposure: 0
        self:
          scene_id: rite.spring.drink.self
          revision: r1
          lines:
            - {kind: narration, text: 石龕裡的水冷得像刀，映出你低頭的臉。}
            - {kind: speech, speaker: actor, text: 「你還在這裡嗎？」}
            - {kind: speech, speaker: host, text: 「喝了，就看得見這片地。」}
            - {kind: narration, text: 水面貼上你的脣，像有一隻手從底下扶住你。}
        watch:
          scene_id: rite.spring.drink.watch
          revision: r1
          lines:
            - {kind: narration, text: 艾蓮俯身，石龕的水面映出她的臉。}
            - {kind: speech, speaker: host, text: 「讓她喝，她就會看見你們要走的路。」}
            - {kind: narration, text: 她的肩沉下去，水面在她脣邊碎開。}
districts:
  border_source_highlands: spring
```

The example above is a shape example, not authored content: the shipped
document carries complete scenes for every option, and the values shown are the
authored bounds rather than placeholders.

### 6.2 Fields and bounds

| Field | Type | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `archetypes` | mapping | 1..16 entries |
| `guardian_name_zh` | string | 1..32 code points |
| `guardian_form_zh` | string | 1..200 code points |
| `options` | sequence | 2..3 entries; keys unique within the archetype |
| `label_zh` | string | 1..32 code points |
| `seconds` | int | 120..900 |
| `shame` | int | 0..2 ordered-level steps |
| `exposure` | int | 0..2 ordered-level steps |
| `self` | scene document | `Scene` bounds (1..32 lines, 8192 canonical bytes) plus content rules in §11 |
| `watch` | scene document or null | same bounds; null is a solo-only ritual that refuses companion performance |
| `districts` | mapping | every district key in `content/maps/districts.yaml` exactly once |

`Scene`, `Speech`, and `Narration` come from `world/lore/scenes.py`. The rite
validation deliberately does **not** apply the dialogue rule that a formal
scene opens with an actor utterance: a rite may open with narration. Its own
rules are: at least one narration line, speakers restricted to `actor` and
`host`, and the content rules in §11.

The archetype's art subject key is derived as `rite_<archetype>`; it is not
stored, so the subject and the archetype key cannot drift apart.

## 7. Completion record

### 7.1 Storage

`PlayerCharacter` attribute `landmark_rites`, schema 1:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "completed": {
    "border_source_highlands": {
      "first_tick": 8123,
      "last_tick": 9044,
      "count": 2,
      "options": {
        "drink": {"count": 1, "last_tick": 8123},
        "bathe": {"count": 1, "last_tick": 9044}
      }
    }
  }
}
```

- `world/rules/landmark_rite_record.py` is the sole writer and the reader.
- Envelope validation is strict: exactly `schema_version` and `completed`;
  `schema_version` must be the integer `1`; district keys are non-empty strings;
  `first_tick <= last_tick`; `count >= 1`; every option entry has integer
  `count >= 1` and `last_tick`.
- A stored district key or option key that the current content no longer
  declares is ignored for display and **preserved** on write, so editing
  content never corrupts a record.
- A shape violation raises `LandmarkRiteRecordError`. The record is never
  overwritten; the ritual refuses with `rite_record_corrupt` and logs a
  diagnostic. Repair belongs to the GM console.
- No migration. Development databases are reset; a pre-existing `map_knowledge`
  visit no longer lights anything.

### 7.2 Write point

The write happens inside the rite settlement transaction, after the time
advance and the sexual-state writes, and targets the record owner. Repeating a
rite increments `count` and the option's `count` and refreshes `last_tick`;
`first_tick` never changes.

## 8. District lighting

`world/rules/map_district_lighting.py` becomes:

```python
def lit_district_keys(character, registry=None) -> frozenset[str]:
    """The districts whose rite this character has completed at least once."""
```

- The lit set is exactly the `completed` keys that the current district
  registry declares.
- Reading lighting writes nothing and creates no attribute. No `map_knowledge`
  read is needed.
- A corrupt record raises `LandmarkRiteRecordError`; the presentation layer
  degrades to an empty lit set and logs, matching today's corrupt-knowledge
  behaviour.

### 8.1 Reveal projection

`KnowledgeSnapshot.revealed_in_bounds()` currently derives the lit set itself.
It becomes a pure projection that accepts the lit set:

```python
def revealed_in_bounds(self, bounds, *, lit: frozenset[str] = frozenset(),
                        obj: str = "") -> frozenset[tuple[int, int]]:
```

`revealed_in_bounds(character, bounds)` resolves the lit set from the rite
record and passes it in. `web/webclient/presentation/area_map.py`, which calls
the snapshot directly, resolves it the same way. `map_knowledge` therefore
remains a view of visits only and never imports the rite record.

Consumers that already call `lit_district_keys()` — `sight.py` distant markers,
`travel_events.py`, `travel_interrupts.py` — need no change beyond the flag
removal in §10.

## 9. Settlement

### 9.1 Action

`explore.landmark_rite` is registered in the webclient action registry and
dispatched by the browser and by the text command. Request:

| Field | Type | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `option` | string | one of the current archetype's option keys |
| `member_id` | int or null | present party companion that performs the rite; null means the controlled character |

The server never trusts a district, archetype, guardian, cost, or scene
identifier supplied by the client. It resolves the district from the record
owner's current canonical node and the archetype from the content document.

### 9.2 Preflight (read-only, zero writes)

1. The request is well formed and the caller controls the record owner.
2. The record owner's current canonical node equals a rite landmark's canonical
   node. Node resolution shares the implementation behind
   `map_knowledge._derive_node_id`, exposed as a public read.
3. The acting body is the controlled character, or a `member_id` naming a
   present, living, non-sleeping party companion.
4. The option exists on that district's archetype, and a companion acting body
   is used only when that option declares a `watch` scene.
5. The record envelope is readable.
6. The actor is not in combat, not in a live dialogue session, and not asleep.
7. `admit_contact_before_action` and `evaluate_skip_safety` admit the action.
8. `planned_seconds` predicts the full declared `seconds`; a shorter interval
   would mean a forced-sleep or registered-event boundary inside the rite.

Any failure raises a stable refusal before any write.

The action carries the dispatcher's request identity. A duplicate click or a
retried transport request replays the cached result instead of settling a
second rite.

### 9.3 Commit (one `transaction.atomic`)

1. Re-validate the district, option, acting body, and readable record under the
   transaction.
2. Apply the canonical climax sequence to the acting body:
   `apply_pleasure_gain(body, 100)` then `apply_pleasure_gain(body, 0)`, the
   documented two-call pattern that walks the phase into `進行中`.
3. Apply the shame and exposure steps through the same ordered-level trait
   writes the settlement path uses.
4. `advance_wait(acting_body, option.seconds)`. The advance's settlement loop closes
   the climax: `climax_ends`, pleasure reset, `climax_today + 1`, experience and
   milestones per the existing rules. Ordering matters: pleasure is applied
   before the advance so the same settlement both starts and closes the
   orgasm inside the rite's own time interval.
5. Write the completion record.
6. Publish, after commit: the `rite` panel with the delivered scene, the
   recomputed map panels, and the `landmark_rite_committed` info event.

One option yields at most one climax. Multiple climaxes would require multiple
advances in one transaction, each publishing its own travel and boundary
events; that cost is not paid here.

### 9.4 Acting body versus record owner

- Sexual state, climax counters, and experience are written to the acting body
  only.
- The record, map knowledge, lit districts, and the `landmark_rite_committed`
  event's `char` all use the record owner.
- Time advances the whole present party, exactly as an ordinary wait does.
- A companion whose `member_id` names it must be present at the landmark; a
  remote or sleeping companion refuses with `rite_member_unavailable`.
- The record owner's own body must be co-located with the acting body. A
  possessed or puppeted mismatch refuses with `rite_unavailable`; there is no
  remote rite.
- A companion rite whose option has no `watch` scene refuses with
  `rite_no_watch_scene`. This stays a permanent authored outcome, not a
  rollout shim: a solo-only ritual is a legitimate declaration.

## 10. Removal of movement-time lighting signals

District lighting no longer changes on movement, so two existing surfaces
become dead and are removed in the lighting change:

1. `district_lit` leaves the `wilderness-travel-interrupts` flag order, the
   webclient `INTERRUPT_TEXT`/`travel_log` validators, and the fixtures and
   browser tests that assert it.
2. `landmark_reached` loses its `lit_district` parameter. The notice keeps
   `arrival_node`, `name`, `prose`, and `artwork`; the travel log line keeps
   `抵達<name>。`, and `LandmarkCard` loses its `◆ <district>已探明` row.

The map payload already announces a newly lit district when the committed map
panel changes, so no replacement movement flag is introduced.

## 11. Adult content and authoring rules

1. The guardian is never male or male-figured. It may be a woman, a non-human
   presence, or a sexless force bound to the landmark.
2. No ritual depicts sex with a male or male-figured being. Penetration or any
   other form is allowed between the acting body and the guardian, the
   landmark object, or a force the landmark manifests, and may address the
   clitoris, labia, urethra, anus, vagina, cervix, and uterus.
3. Every option must give a diegetic reason for all three beats: **gaining
   information**, **interacting with the landmark object at the landmark**, and
   **the climax**. Typical shapes: the guardian trades a truth for a touch; the
   object answers a question only while it is being used; the climax is what
   the landmark consumes or registers. Information is shown through what the
   guardian does and what the object reveals, never stated as "you learn that".
4. Show, do not tell. No "你得知了", no "你感到非常興奮"; use action, sound,
   object response, and body response.
5. The `self` scene is second person and assumes a female acting body. The
   `watch` scene is third person, refers to the companion **only by name**, and
   uses neither 她 nor 他, so a companion of any gender reads correctly.
6. Scenes are Traditional Chinese, full-width punctuation, JRPG sword-and-sorcery
   vocabulary. No 武俠 vocabulary, no OOC, no mention of a command, mechanic, or
   UI element.
7. Each scene declares a stable `scene_id` and a `revision`; the revision is
   bumped whenever shipped lines change.
8. The information beat does not write the lore codex. The existing arrival
   lore reveal for anchors and regions is unchanged and independent of rites.

## 12. Presentation

### 12.1 `rite` panel

A new presentation panel `rite`, schema version 1, registered beside
`dialogue` and `travel_log`:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "available": true,
  "kind": "rite",
  "district": {"key": "border_source_highlands", "name": "界源高地",
               "landmark": "源泉石龕"},
  "guardian": {"name": "泉之聲", "form": "水面映出的聲音，只在有人俯身時開口。"},
  "art": {"subject": "rite_spring", "attempts": []},
  "options": [{"key": "drink", "label": "俯身飲下", "seconds": 180}],
  "delivery": {
    "kind": "formal",
    "delivery_id": "rite:border_source_highlands:drink:8123",
    "scene_id": "rite.spring.drink.self",
    "revision": "r1",
    "actor_name": "你",
    "guardian_name": "泉之聲",
    "lines": [{"kind": "narration", "text": "石龕裡的水冷得像刀，映出你低頭的臉。"}]
  },
  "seen": ["drink"]
}
```

- Availability is decided solely by the record owner's current canonical node
  matching a rite landmark. It is available lit or unlit, in a wilderness cell
  or in a settlement room, which is what puts 王都中央廣場 in scope without a
  second surface.
- `delivery` is the last committed rite's scene, held in the record owner's
  `ndb` as presentation state. It is nullable; a reconnect without it shows the
  options without a scene, and the player may perform the rite again.
- `art` is the archetype's scene descriptor through the existing resolution
  chain, or a quiet ink frame.
- `seen` is the option-key list from the record for the current district.
- Bounds: `options` 1..3, `delivery` within the `Scene` canonical-byte bound,
  `delivery_id` opaque ASCII of 1..128 characters and unique per committed rite,
  `seen` 1..3, `art` within the existing descriptor bounds.

### 12.2 Webclient

- The card mounts in the choices anchor. It carries the archetype art, the
  guardian's name and form, the option buttons, and 退開. It renders no prose
  lines of its own.
- Selecting an option dispatches `explore.landmark_rite`; on success the store
  adopts the returned delivery as the active scene source.
- Scene playback is the **existing** shared player, not a second renderer:
  `composables/use-authored-scene.js` owns the line cursor and
  `components/MessageWindow.vue` owns pages, the typewriter, the speaker plate,
  click/Space/Enter progression, reduced motion, and key retirement. The
  delivery satisfies `isAuthoredDelivery` (`kind` is `formal`, `delivery_id`
  and `lines` are present), and the shell supplies `actorName` (the acting
  body) and `hostName` (the guardian's name). No rite-specific paging code
  exists.
- A rite scene and a dialogue scene are mutually exclusive: the action refuses
  inside a live dialogue session, and the shell presents the rite source only
  when no dialogue session is live.
- On a rite landmark, the rite card replaces the generic `LandmarkCard` for the
  arrival that produced the `landmark_reached` notice. The travel log still
  records the arrival line.
- After the delivery ends, the card returns to the option list. Once the
  lighting change has landed, the district is lit and the world map announces
  it from the recomputed panel.
- Leaving the landmark makes the panel unavailable and drops the card.

### 12.3 Text client

New command:

| Syntax | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `儀式` | Lists the landmark's guardian, its options, and which have been experienced. |
| `儀式 <選項>` | Performs the option with the controlled character. |
| `儀式 <選項> <隊友>` | Performs the option with the named present companion. |

The text client emits the complete scene at once, in order, and then the
completion line naming the district. A complete emission is the whole delivery;
there is no per-line pager. The command shares the same action, so refusal codes
and settlement are identical to the browser. A companion name that is missing
or matches more than one present member refuses with `rite_member_unavailable`.
`docs/game/commands.md` and `docs/game/command-reference.md` are updated in the
same change.

## 13. Error handling

| Condition | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Content document invalid | Snapshot rejected at load with named diagnostics (file, field path, code) and startup refuses adoption |
| Record envelope corrupt | Read raises; lit set degrades to empty with `landmark_rite_record_corrupt`; the ritual refuses `rite_record_corrupt` and never overwrites |
| Not on a rite landmark | `rite_no_landmark`, zero writes |
| Unknown option | `rite_unknown_option`, zero writes |
| Acting body unavailable | `rite_member_unavailable`, zero writes |
| Companion rite on a solo-only option | `rite_no_watch_scene`, zero writes |
| Combat, dialogue, sleep | `rite_unavailable`, zero writes |
| Skip-safety refusal | `rite_unsafe`, zero writes |
| Predicted interval shorter than declared | `rite_interrupted`, zero writes |
| Any exception after the first write | The transaction rolls back; no time, sexual state, or record survives |
| Missing or failed art | Quiet ink frame, never substitute art |
| Panel payload over bound | Panel renders unavailable; the action still works |

## 18. Risks and rejected alternatives

**Risks**

- **Content volume is the real cost.** Forty scenes (ten archetypes × two
  options × two perspectives) at four to six lines each, plus review, is larger
  than the mechanism. Splitting content into later changes keeps each change
  shippable.
- **Map progress becomes slower.** A district now requires a deliberate act at
  its landmark. The panel appears automatically on arrival and the command
  works anywhere on the node, so the affordance is not hidden.
- **Repeatable rites can farm sexual state.** Only time and arousal penalties
  are paid, and no economic reward exists, so the farming ceiling is the
  player's own clock. This is a deliberate bound; a throttle was rejected
  because it adds a persisted counter and test surface for no economic reason.
- **A corrupt record blocks that character's lighting.** This matches the
  existing corrupt-knowledge discipline and is repaired by the GM console.
- **Wire changes.** Removing `district_lit` and `lit_district` breaks fixtures
  and browser tests; the removal change updates every consumer in one commit.

**Rejected alternatives**

- **In-scene choice branches.** The immutable scene model forbids in-scene
  branches and executable statements; interaction lives on the panel.
- **LLM-generated per-landmark scenes.** Breaks the offline-playable invariant
  and produces two experiences.
- **Per-landmark bespoke scenes.** 120 × 2 perspectives is not reviewable.
- **A global adult-content toggle.** A separate feature with its own storage
  and UI; declining a rite per visit needs no state.
- **Keeping `district_lit` for a city exception.** Exceptions multiply; the
  panel's node-based availability already serves the city.
